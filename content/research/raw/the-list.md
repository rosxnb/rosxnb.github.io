## Digital Stamp

With increasing number of AI contents (like picture, videos, music, code, ...),
can a system capable of stamping any generated digital stream (pdf, png, mp3, mp4, text, ...) be programmed?

The properties of the digital stamp:

- **Harmless during integration**: when adding the stamp to a digital stream, the digital stream stays uncorrupted. Meaning, the
digital stream should be recognized by its hosting application and be able to execute / parse the digital stream successfully.

- **Harmfull with force removal**: force removal of digital stamp corrupts the digital stream making the content garbage.
